# 上图生成提示词

填入观察到的图2场景。只生成上图，随后用原始图2进行确定性拼接。

> Generate ONE photorealistic photograph, not a diptych or collage. Reference image 1 defines person A, the photographer. Preserve A's distinct facial geometry, hair, skin tone and apparent age. Reference image 2 is an already finished photograph of person B: use it only to infer the setting and photographic language for this new reverse shot. Do not recreate or output B's finished photograph.
>
> Show A alone, chest or waist up, actively taking reference image 2 of B, who is just outside the frame near the viewer. Observe A from an external reverse-shot viewpoint close to B. Hold a compact vintage camera in a believable active shooting posture, lens aimed toward B. Let head angle, camera design and shooting direction determine the camera position and facial occlusion naturally: left side, right side, half face, most of the face or the entire face may be covered. Do not prescribe a visible eye or force the camera aside to display the face. Align the chosen eye to the actual viewfinder; if using a camera screen, lower the camera naturally and direct the gaze at the screen. Use a relaxed coherent shoulder/elbow/wrist posture, a plausible shutter finger and supporting hand. Preserve identity in whatever features remain visible, or through hair, head silhouette, skin and build when the face is fully covered. No eyes visible through the camera body and no unnatural pose solely to reveal identity.
>
> Scene inferred from image 2: {location, time, weather, spatial cues}. Match its {lighting, grading, lens impression, grain}, allowing perspective-correct reverse-shot background changes. Do not invent boats or other conspicuous scenery absent from image 2. Style A's outfit to coordinate with B's existing {clothing colors/material/details}; B's appearance, clothes, expression and original scene content must not be redrawn; the original will only be cropped and proportionally resized for layout.
>
> COLOR AND CAPTURE MATCH: Treat image 2 as the sole authoritative color-grade and exposure reference. Image 1 supplies identity only, never its original lighting or color. Match image 2's {white balance/tint, exposure, black point, contrast, saturation, highlight roll-off, shadow detail, skin rendering, grain, flash presence or absence}. In particular reproduce {observed sky gradient, terrain hues, shadow density}. The pair must feel captured at the same place and moment with the same camera and filter. Do not brighten the face independently, warm the image, add fill flash, or apply a cinematic grade absent from image 2. Infer physically plausible reverse-shot light direction rather than forcing identical face brightness or background pixels. Only the new upper photograph may be corrected for color matching; leave the original lower photograph's grade untouched.
>
> GENTLE LOCAL LINE RETOUCH: If A has faint forehead lines or nasolabial lines in the generated upper image, lightly soften or remove only those fine lines or overly dark crease shadows for a flattering natural portrait, especially lines inadvertently exaggerated by generation. Preserve real expression folds, nose/mouth anatomy, cheek volume, apparent age, subtle skin texture, tonal variation and identity marks. Do not flatten the nasolabial area or demand a completely line-free face. This is a narrow exception to no-retouch guidance, not permission for full-face smoothing, whitening or de-aging. Keep lighting and matched colors, and never retouch B's original photograph.
>
> SUBTLE CONTOUR REFINEMENT: Keep A immediately recognizable with the original face shape, jaw width, cheekbones, feature proportions and apparent age. Very gently smooth only unnatural generated bumps or abrupt cheek-to-jaw transitions, favoring flattering but plausible head angle and light. No noticeable slimming, bone reshaping, pointed chin, enlarged eyes, altered nose/mouth or forced symmetry. Keep natural facial volume, small identity marks and real skin texture. Apply only to A, never to B's original.
>
> SHOOTING GEOMETRY: Before generation infer image2's viewpoint from horizon, pitch, face/body orientation, gaze and perspective. Distinguish a turned torso looking back at the camera from a genuine profile view. Position A and camera at a plausible height, distance and pitch that could produce image2, with the external observer close to B. A's lens axis must aim toward offscreen B and the viewfinder gaze, camera body, arms and stance must agree. Do not default to frontal eye-level if image2 is clearly high/low angle. B need not look at the lens. Allow perspective-correct reverse background and light-direction changes; do not blindly duplicate a sunset behind both opposite views. Unknown focal length/location may be plausibly estimated, not claimed exact. Validate the pair's spatial relationship; fix only the upper viewpoint or pose if inconsistent, preserving B's photo.
>
> EYE AND PUPIL ANATOMY: Every visible eye must have a natural iris and pupil contained within it, coherent eyelids and sclera, and physically plausible size and placement for gaze, perspective and light. No duplicated/double pupils, displaced black dots, malformed irises, implausible pupil sizes, contradictory gaze or extra pupils created from flash reflections. Both eyes, if visible, coordinate naturally without forced symmetry. The viewing eye aligns with the real viewfinder; gaze serves shooting, not the external viewer. The other eye may naturally remain open or gently close. Keep occluded eye portions hidden; never draw an eye through camera or hand and never force exposure of both eyes. Preserve A's natural eye shape and identity. Inspect visible eyes after generation; if anatomically wrong, correct only the upper eye region without changing identity, occlusion, pose, skin texture or matched colors, then recheck before assembly.
>
> RELAXED NECK AND SHOULDERS: Keep the neck naturally extended and shoulders dropped and relaxed, chest uncompressed, chin comfortably clear of collar. Raise forearms and adjust camera height to meet the eye rather than shrugging shoulders up toward the camera. Elbows hang or open gently, wrists stay relaxed. No hunched neck, tucked chin, raised shoulders or compressed upper body; no artificial long neck or rigid military posture.
>
> PHYSICAL REALISM: Prioritize a believable casual photograph over polished perfection. Use relaxed body weight and slightly asymmetric shoulders. Infer actual light sources from image 2 and the reverse-shot position: face, hands, camera and clothing must share plausible light direction, color and intensity. Render natural camera/hand occlusion shadows on the face and subtle contact shadows where fingers touch the camera. No unexplained fill light, glowing skin, artificial rim outlines or inconsistent multiple shadows. Hands must have correct anatomical proportions and joint bends, relaxed overlapping fingers with believable grip pressure, shutter finger near shutter, thumb behind the body, supporting hand beneath camera or lens. No fused/extra fingers, floating palms, penetrating fingertips, rubbery skin, evenly posed fingers or both hands blocking the front of the lens. Naturally hidden fingers need not all be visible. Do not hide artifacts with added blur, grain or darkness. Preserve ordinary skin tonal variation without exaggerated texture or retouch.
>
> NATURAL HAIR: Preserve A's original haircut, length, density and growth pattern. Wind is optional and subtle. For short hair keep the main silhouette settled, with only a few small loose hairs moving gently, never a dramatic windswept crown. For long hair use plausible gravity, soft bending and uneven natural clumps. Infer wind physically from image 2 and relative viewpoints, not identical screen-left/right movement; do not apply long-hair movement to short hair. Avoid radial spikes, porcupine hair, floating tufts, wire-like isolated strands, evenly spaced flyaways or disconnected roots. No enhanced strand detail. Prefer understated hair to an artificial wind effect.
>
> NATURAL PHOTO TEXTURE AND UNRETOUCHED SKIN: Use ordinary snapshot focus with finite lens resolution and gentle natural transitions, judged at the final panel's display scale, not by whether every detail survives magnification. Keep face, hands and camera understandable without crisp microdetail, hard contours, clarity enhancement, sharpening, HDR or commercial retouching. Preserve subtle uneven skin color, faint natural texture, small existing imperfections and identity marks on the face and hands, with realistic nonuniform reflections. No beauty filter, skin smoothing, blemish removal, whitening, waxy/plastic/porcelain skin, aggressive denoising or broad featureless skin patches. Do not overcorrect with exaggerated pores, wrinkles or invented blemishes. Natural texture should remain understated, never sharpened to prove realism. Soft lens rendering is distinct from skin smoothing: retain skin tonal variation without added blur. No artificial defocus, grain overlay or darkening to hide artifacts. With direct flash preserve natural tonal transitions rather than uniformly glowing skin; do not copy accidental skin detail loss from an overexposed reference. If the upper image looks too crisp or smoothed, edit only its rendering and skin while preserving identity, pose, lighting and matched colors. Do not redraw the lower original.
>
> Target upper-panel aspect ratio: landscape 3:2 by default (or user-specified shared landscape panel ratio). Frame the head, camera and hands with comfortable margins. Natural candid photographic texture. No extra people, text, borders or watermark. Generate only A's new photograph; the original image 2 will be appended separately using only cropping and proportional resizing, with no generative changes, followed by a restrained non-generative global color adjustment of the assembled diptych.

若用户明确要求上图两人入镜，将主体段改成侧后方中远景：A 正在为 B 拍照，保留两人身份和图2中 B 的造型。其余生成与原图拼接流程不变。

## 拼接后处理

在上图与图2完成确定性拼接后，按 SKILL.md 的“拼接后整体微调”进行一次克制的全局调色。不得将整个拼图重新生成为图像，也不得承诺下图最终颜色逐像素不变。

拼接前，先以下图实际裁切区域为目标单独校准上图色调；比较天空、海面/地面及肤色，修正色偏、曝光和对比度后，再进行拼接与轻微全局统一。相同的全局滤镜不能替代上图色差修正。

校色验收：至少比较两类对应环境/材质的亮度与色偏，记录具体问题与本次参数，不用固定饱和度/亮度系数。上图校色后必须生成临时拼图检查；仍像两套滤镜就继续修正上图，最后全局调色仅作收尾。
